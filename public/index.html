    Learn the game

    The king needs a way out.

    Two people share this board. The dark attackers surround the ivory defenders and their crowned king.

    Two sides, two goals

    Attackers: capture the king by surrounding him on four sides with attackers or hostile special squares, or encircle every defender.

    Defenders: move the king to any corner, or form an exit fort: a mobile king at the edge protected by a formation that survives capture analysis.

    Move & capture

    Every piece moves any clear distance horizontally or vertically. Pieces cannot jump. Only the king may stop on the center throne or a corner; other pieces may cross an empty throne.

    Sandwich an enemy piece between your moved piece and friendly support to capture it. Special squares can also provide hostile support. An empty throne is hostile to both sides; a throne occupied by the king is hostile to attackers. Captures happen automatically. Supported groups along a physical board edge can be captured by a shieldwall; this does not capture the king.

    Repeated positions

    A second occurrence is allowed. The third occurrence of the same position and side to move ends the game as an attacker win. Captures restart repetition history. A direct board win takes precedence. Repeated destinations show ↻; a losing repeat also shows ! and remains playable.

    At the board

    Select a piece belonging to the side to move, then a highlighted destination. Select it again or press Esc to cancel. Arrow keys explore the board; Enter or Space selects a piece or plays a move. Home and End move to a row's edges; Ctrl+Home and Ctrl+End reach the board's corners.

    These are this project's implemented rules. There is no pass or separate no-legal-move ending. The current local game, its variants and review position are saved automatically in this browser and restored when you return. Starting or loading another game replaces that automatic copy. Use Save / copy to keep separate records. Load accepts portable game notation or a JSON variant study. Choose a file or drop one into the load dialog to review its contents before loading.

    Review & alternatives

    Use the controls below the board to step back or forward, go to the opening or return to the end. Select a move in the record to jump to its position. Recorded future moves stay intact. To try a different continuation, select a piece and destination from an earlier position. The Create a variant dialog identifies your move and lets you add a description. Confirm to create the variant and play the move, or cancel to leave the game unchanged. The Variants tab lists your lines. At a fork, Next offers each continuation and its description. Saving a game with variants preserves the whole study as JSON, including your current review position.

    Move comments

    Select a move to read its comments below the record or add a comment of your own. Local comments are anonymous. Imported comments may name an author with {[Alice]:text}; ordinary colons do not identify an author. Names in [brackets] are highlighted mentions. Hover, focus or tap a reference such as <D3> or <D1-D4> to mark it temporarily on the board. Imported comments longer than 500 characters are shortened and labeled Truncated.
